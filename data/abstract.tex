\begin{cabstract}
多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）融合视觉与语言等多模态能力，巨大的参数量和计算量使其训练过程高度依赖分布式并行计算。面向真实场景时，GPU 集群通常由多代异构设备组成，显存、算力与互联带宽的代际差异明显，训练系统难以在不同设备间实现稳定高效的资源协同。与此同时，MLLMs 由编码器、投影层与 LLM 基座等异构子模块构成，并普遍采用分阶段冻结训练，导致不同训练阶段的计算负载与显存需求差异显著且会动态变化。现有并行训练方法多基于同构 GPU 集群的前提，而且没有联合考虑 MLLMs 的模块结构、阶段性冻结行为与异构设备特性，导致在异构 GPU 集群下训练 MLLMs 时容易出现负载不均衡、计算通信冗余以及 GPU 利用率低的问题，进而限制训练吞吐。

针对异构 GPU 集群与分阶段冻结训练下并行策略固定、易出现负载不均衡与冗余计算通信的问题，本文提出 MMoH 框架。该方法通过需求驱动的设备拓扑识别、复合粒度模型抽象与冻结感知模型划分，自动生成面向不同训练阶段的混合并行策略，使并行配置能够随训练阶段动态调整。进一步地，MMoH 通过提前终止调度机制消除连续冻结流水级中的冗余反向计算与通信，降低不必要的同步开销。实验在两种异构 GPU 集群与两种规模的 MLLMs（3B、12B）上开展，结果表明 MMoH 在多种冻结场景下相较 Megatron-LM、Whale 与 Espresso 均取得性能提升，最高加速约 1.77 倍，验证了方法在异构环境中的有效性。

针对现有全局统一重计算策略在异构 GPU 集群上难以兼顾 GPU 利用率与显存开销的问题，本文在 MMoH 基础上进一步提出 HR-MMoH。该方法在 MMoH 划分与调度结果基础上，按流水级进行差异化重计算配置，并结合剖析驱动的配置搜索形成“划分—调度—重计算”的协同优化方法，从而在不同设备与不同阶段间实现更细粒度的性能-显存权衡。在更大的批次大小与序列长度的端到端实验中，HR-MMoH 在不同冻结场景下均取得最大吞吐，迭代时间相比 Espresso 缩短约 6.1\%--23.1\%，相对 Whale 的可训练子集缩短约 11.0\%--40.6\%。上述结果表明，HR-MMoH 能够进一步提升异构 GPU 集群训练 MLLMs 的速度。
\end{cabstract}
\ckeywords{多模态大语言模型；异构 GPU 集群；流水线并行；重计算}

\begin{eabstract}
Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) integrate multiple modalities such as vision and language, and their massive parameter counts and computational demands make training highly dependent on distributed parallel computing. In real-world scenarios, GPU clusters are usually composed of heterogeneous devices from multiple generations, with substantial differences in memory capacity, compute capability, and interconnect bandwidth, which makes it difficult for training systems to achieve stable and efficient resource coordination across devices. Meanwhile, MLLMs are composed of heterogeneous submodules, including encoders, projectors, and LLM backbones, and are commonly trained with stage-wise freezing. As a result, the computational workload and memory demand vary significantly across training stages and change dynamically over time. Existing parallel training methods are mostly designed for homogeneous GPU clusters and do not jointly consider the modular structure of MLLMs, stage-wise freezing behavior, and hardware heterogeneity. Consequently, training MLLMs on heterogeneous GPU clusters often suffers from load imbalance, redundant computation and communication, and low GPU utilization, which ultimately limits training throughput.

To address the problems of fixed parallel strategies, load imbalance, and redundant computation and communication under heterogeneous GPU clusters with stage-wise freezing, this thesis proposes the MMoH framework. MMoH automatically generates hybrid parallel strategies for different training stages through demand-driven device topology identification, compound-granularity model abstraction, and freeze-aware model partitioning, allowing the parallel configuration to adapt dynamically as the training stage changes. Furthermore, MMoH introduces an early-termination scheduling mechanism to eliminate redundant backward computation and communication in consecutively frozen pipeline stages, thereby reducing unnecessary synchronization overhead. Experiments are conducted on two heterogeneous GPU clusters and two MLLM scales (3B and 12B). The results show that, across multiple freezing scenarios, MMoH outperforms Megatron-LM, Whale, and Espresso, achieving speedups of up to 1.77$\times$, which verifies its effectiveness in heterogeneous environments.

To address the difficulty of globally uniform recomputation strategies in simultaneously balancing GPU utilization and memory overhead on heterogeneous GPU clusters, this thesis further proposes HR-MMoH on top of MMoH. Based on the partitioning and scheduling results of MMoH, HR-MMoH applies differentiated recomputation configurations at the pipeline-stage level and combines them with profiling-guided configuration search, forming a coordinated optimization approach over partitioning, scheduling, and recomputation. In this way, it enables finer-grained performance-memory trade-offs across different devices and training stages. In end-to-end experiments with larger batch sizes and sequence lengths, HR-MMoH achieves the highest throughput under different freezing scenarios. Compared with Espresso, it reduces iteration time by approximately 6.1\%--23.1\%; compared with the trainable subset of Whale, it reduces iteration time by approximately 11.0\%--40.6\%. These results indicate that HR-MMoH can further accelerate MLLM training on heterogeneous GPU clusters.
\end{eabstract}
