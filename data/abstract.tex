\begin{cabstract}
多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）融合视觉与语言等多模态能力，巨大的参数量和计算量使其训练过程高度依赖分布式并行计算。面向真实场景时，GPU 集群通常由多代异构设备组成，显存、算力的代际差异明显。与此同时，MLLMs 由编码器、投影层与 LLM 基座等异构子模块构成，并普遍采用分阶段冻结训练，导致不同训练阶段的计算负载与显存需求动态变化。现有并行训练方法多基于同构 GPU 集群的前提，并且没有考虑 MLLMs 的异构模块结构与分阶段冻结训练，难以解决异构 GPU 环境下的负载均衡、冻结阶段存在冗余计算与通信以及显存安全与计算效率协同优化的问题。

针对异构 GPU 集群与分阶段冻结训练下并行策略固定、易出现负载不均衡与冗余反向通信的问题，本文提出研究方法一：MMoH。该方法通过需求驱动的设备拓扑识别、复合粒度模型抽象与冻结感知整数规划划分，自动生成面向不同训练阶段的混合并行策略，并通过提前终止调度机制裁剪连续冻结流水级中的冗余反向计算与流水级间通信。在两种异构 GPU 集群与两种规模的 MLLMs（3B、12B）上的实验表明，MMoH 在多种冻结场景下相较 Megatron-LM、Whale 与 Espresso 均取得最高吞吐，最高加速约 1.77 倍，且提前终止调度可带来约 8\%--19\% 性能增益。

针对现有全局统一重计算策略在异构集群上难以兼顾显存安全与计算效率的问题，本文在 MMoH 的基础上提出研究方法二：HR-MMoH。该方法在 MMoH 划分与调度结果基础上，按流水级进行差异化重计算配置，并结合剖析驱动的配置搜索形成 “划分—调度—重计算” 的协同优化方法。在更大的批次大小与序列长度的端到端实验中，HR-MMoH 在 no-freeze、freeze-encoder、freeze-llm、freeze-both 四种场景下均取得最大吞吐，迭代时间相比 Espresso 缩短约 6.1\%--23.1\%，相对 Whale 的可训练子集缩短约 11.0\%--40.6\%。
\end{cabstract}
\ckeywords{多模态大语言模型；异构 GPU 集群；流水线并行；重计算}

\begin{eabstract}
Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) integrate vision-language capabilities and demand substantial computation at scale, making training highly reliant on distributed parallelism. In practical deployments, GPU clusters are typically heterogeneous across generations, with pronounced disparities in memory capacity and compute throughput. At the same time, MLLMs consist of heterogeneous submodules (e.g., encoders, projectors, and LLM backbones) and are commonly optimized with stage-wise freezing, which induces stage-dependent shifts in workload and memory pressure. Existing parallel training methods are largely designed for homogeneous clusters and do not explicitly model either the heterogeneous module structure of MLLMs or the dynamics introduced by stage-wise freezing. Consequently, they cannot effectively and jointly address cross-device load balancing, redundant computation/communication during freezing stages, and the trade-off between memory safety and computational efficiency.

To mitigate fixed parallel strategies under heterogeneous clusters and stage-wise freezing, this thesis proposes Method I: MMoH. MMoH combines demand-driven topology identification, compound-granularity model abstraction, and freeze-aware integer-programming partitioning to automatically derive stage-adaptive hybrid parallel strategies. It further introduces an early-termination scheduler to prune redundant backward computation and inter-stage communication in consecutively frozen pipeline stages. Experiments on two heterogeneous GPU clusters and two MLLM scales (3B and 12B) demonstrate that MMoH consistently achieves the highest throughput among Megatron-LM, Whale, and Espresso across multiple freezing settings, with up to 1.77$\times$ speedup. The early-termination scheduler contributes an additional 8\%--19\% performance gain.

To overcome the limitations of globally uniform recomputation on heterogeneous clusters, this thesis further proposes Method II: HR-MMoH, built on top of MMoH. HR-MMoH applies stage-wise differentiated recomputation configurations over MMoH partitioning and scheduling results, and couples them with profiling-guided configuration search, yielding a coordinated optimization framework over partitioning, scheduling, and recomputation. In end-to-end experiments with larger batch sizes and sequence lengths, HR-MMoH achieves the highest throughput in all four settings (no-freeze, freeze-encoder, freeze-llm, and freeze-both). Relative to Espresso, it reduces iteration time by 6.1\%--23.1\%; relative to the trainable subset of Whale, it reduces iteration time by 11.0\%--40.6\%.
\end{eabstract}
\ekeywords{Multimodal large language model; Heterogeneous GPU cluster; Pipeline parallelism; Recomputation}
